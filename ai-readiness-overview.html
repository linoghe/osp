10 AI Readiness Program

Program Overview

A structured engagement for companies that want AI engines to represent them accurately, completely, and helpfully — because they built it that way on purpose.

10.00 The Situation

Your customers are asking AI. Your website isn’t answering.

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude are now a primary discovery layer for customers making considered purchases. When someone asks “who does the best boutique hotel experience for a solo business traveler in Lisbon?” or “which agency does B2B content strategy that actually converts?”, an AI answers. Your SEO strategy, your beautifully designed website, your carefully crafted homepage — none of that was built for this moment.

Most sites fail the AI test for the same three reasons: content isn’t structured around the questions customers actually ask; there’s no machine-readable semantic layer that tells AI what the content means; and there’s no plain-text version an LLM can consume without rendering a browser. Fix all three and your site becomes a reliable, citable source. Leave any one of them broken and the AI makes something up — or skips you entirely.

The opportunity: This is early. Most competitors have not addressed it. The companies that build AI-readable content foundations now will compound that advantage for years. The work required to do it well is exactly the work OSP is built to do.

10.06 Success Metrics

How to measure it

AI readiness metrics are still maturing as an industry practice. These are the most reliable signals available today, with notes on how to track each one.

Primary
AI Citation Accuracy
Ask 10 target queries across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude. Score each response: accurate, incomplete, or wrong. Benchmark before program; retest at 90 days and 6 months.
Primary
AI Citation Frequency
Track how often the client is cited by name in AI answers for target queries. Tools: Perplexity queries, BrandMentions, early AEO features in Semrush / Ahrefs.
Technical
Schema Coverage %
% of key page types with validated schema markup. Measured via Google Rich Results Test and schema.org validator. Target: 100% of priority pages.
Technical
Mirror Freshness
Days since any mirror file was updated vs. days since the source page changed. Target: <7 days lag on key pages. Automated via CI pipeline where available.
Content
Question Coverage
% of the Customer Question Map answered by published content. Reviewed quarterly as new questions emerge and existing content is updated.
Governance
Governance Adherence
% of new content published with schema + Markdown per the checklist. Tracked by the Client PM. Reported quarterly to OSP during retainer.
