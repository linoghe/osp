0.01 Purpose

What this document is for

This is my point of view on how to scope, structure, and price the engagement we've been discussing. It covers the full picture — Jam's schema track, the markdown site mirror I'm recommending, the optional technical implementation, and PM governance.

Everything here is dynamic: the pricing model, effort breakdowns, and timeline all pull live from a shared Google Sheet. Adjust the inputs and the outputs update in real time — day counts, costs, margins, and resource allocation all recalculate automatically.

This is a working document. Tinker with the numbers in the Sheet, walk through the pages, and let me know where you land. I'm here to iterate.
