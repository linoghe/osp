1.03 Discipline

What the Work Covers

A discrete piece of B2B strategy and technical content work at the intersection of:

  • Information architecture and structured-data design (schema.org spec)
  • AI-search visibility and generative-engine-optimization (GEO/AEO) advisory
  • Content design, specifically writing schema-block content values in two languages (DE + EN)
  • Coordination with the client's technical implementation partner on architectural choices, async QA, and deploy-time verification
  • Validation against an agreed pre/post AI-engine query baseline
1.04 Deliverable

What Gets Handed Over

Schema Track

  • A complete, page-by-page schema specification
  • An AI-engine optimization layer (including llms.txt and llms-full.txt recommendations)
  • A comparison report at the end (pre/post AI-engine query results)

Markdown Site Track

  • Machine-readable markdown site mirror (.md tree, DE + EN)
  • Render pipeline specification and acceptance criteria
  • AI-engine citation validation (schema-only vs schema+mirror comparison)
  • Pipeline architecture and maintenance handover
