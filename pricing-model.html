7.03 Notes

How to Use This

  • Dynamic pricing. All costs pull live from the Google Sheet. Adjust rates or zero out days and totals update instantly.
  • Technical costs are optional. If OSP does not subcontract the pipeline build, zero out the Ext-Technical days and those costs disappear.
  • PM rider (15%) is calculated on total program hours including technical, providing governance overhead.
  • Data refreshes on page load. No caching. What's in the sheet is what renders here.
  • Rate reference in the Baseline Matrix tab shows hourly rates and OSP costs per role.
7.04 Setup

Connect Your Google Sheet

To connect your sheet, set the SHEET_ID variable in this page's script to your Google Sheet ID.

Your sheet URL looks like: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/SHEET_ID/edit

Make sure the sheet is published: File → Share → Publish to web → Entire Document → CSV.
